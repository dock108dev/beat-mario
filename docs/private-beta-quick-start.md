# Local app quick start

Use an explicitly built and installed local macOS app, or follow the
[source quickstart](../README.md#launch-and-first-use). This repository does not
provide a notarized public distribution. Retained builds have their own resource
manifests and qualification scope; see [known limitations](known-limitations.md).

## Open the app

Open your installed **Game Companion.app** from Finder. The Games page checks
Codex CLI sign-in, separately installed games and actual capture/input readiness.
Follow the displayed missing-prerequisite remedy. Selected game context/images
are sent for remote inference; authentication remains CLI-managed.

## Stardew ordinary farm

1. Choose Stardew, select a supported farm in the discovered default-save list and approve/open an isolated copy. Discovered farms do not require manual path entry. Explicit selected-folder copying remains an alternate path.
2. Choose **Prepare this copy for watering**. This permits observed loading, bounded bedroom/entrance movement, view settings and tool selection, within five minutes/100 steps and any shorter discussed limit. Stop/Take control cancel preparation. Wait for current-scene readiness and visible resources.
3. Discuss the observed dry crops and your limits. You can correct the selected targets or exclude crops before review. Set one attempt, at most three minutes including return, at most one can unit, and minimum energy/water reserves.
4. Read the current targets, exclusions and finite limits; Review, then Start. Inspect separately confirmed crop effects, resource use, farmhouse return and neutral handback. Input sent alone is not success.

Support is limited to the declared standard spring farmhouse/blue clothing/basic can, readable exact resources, 1–15 visible newly planted seeds in the local patch and recognized clear approaches/return. Uncertain ground blocks movement. Mature crops, arbitrary layouts, distant crops, upgraded tools and refill remain unsupported. Frozen prepared-farm imports and raw controls belong to engineering regression/diagnostics; they are not ordinary preparation instructions.

## Mario early segment

Choose Mario, use the supported local cartridge and discovered FCEUX, and open a fresh disposable companion session. Enter World 1-1 through ordinary menus. Request the supported early segment with finite limits, Review, then Start. The endpoint is alive and grounded at x≥700 followed by neutral acknowledgment and game-owned review pause; it is not full-level completion.

Saved compatible coaching can guide the next attempt. Request a fresh scope after storing new advice so that the review displays it. Matching skill receipts establish application; saved advice alone establishes no gameplay effect or improvement. Stored coaching does not establish successful native application.

## Stop, recovery and history

Stop/Take control revokes continuation and cancels owned inference. Wait for confirmed neutral handback. Changed focus/window/settings, provider failure or disconnect needs fresh observation and review before further gameplay. Switching invalidates prior plans and replies. Canceled/consumed attempts remain recorded; reopening grants no new attempt or approval.

Quit through the app, keyboard or Dock. Active keyboard/Dock cleanup is not yet qualified on the retained build. Cleanup failures are recorded locally in `app-lifecycle.json` and `cleanup-failures.json`; retain failure evidence before retrying. Reopening retains descriptions, results, configuration and compatible guidance, never a live session, plan, review or input authority. Closing a browser is not app shutdown.

Guide & feedback reports remain local; preview them before sharing. Preserve history and credentials through updates. Embedded documentation reflects the build-time source; later repository changes do not update an installed app.
