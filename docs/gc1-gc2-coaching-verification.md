## Delivered contract

The supported experiment is Super Mario Bros. 3 World 1-1's opening hop, from fresh power-on or the runtime's verified opening entry, stopping at x ≥ 160. Natural supported practice requests propose a finite one-to-five attempt budget (default three) over at most ten minutes. Contextual yes or reviewed Start grants the proposed scope. Every trial is capped at three minutes and the shared approval deadline.

Timing feedback adjusts an integer delay from zero to twelve frames before the existing 26-frame jump hold. It applies to a later compatible attempt; the current jump is unchanged. Original words, old/new parameters and controller events are retained. Explicit Try again uses remembered guidance and fresh disposable power-on without another Start inside the remaining scope. It verifies release, same cartridge, new process/observation and cancellation before gameplay. It never performs unattended retries, writes game RAM or loads a personal save/savestate.

Guidance survives reopening in `artifacts/conversation/coaching.json`; plans/input authority do not. Reset clears future guidance and retains outcomes. Urgent Stop/Take control bypass conversation/action serialization, cancel pending planning/retry preparation, and require renewed approval. Existing accepted routes, registry and historical artifacts are preserved.

Outcome receipts distinguish controller application, reached stop, death/partial result and input release. Comparison requires the same cartridge, fresh-power-on entry, opening contract and playback rate. A prior death followed by stop completion is narrowly observed completion improvement with causation unknown; equal completions do not prove improvement. Incompatible or incomplete observations remain unknown.
