## Control and freshness boundaries

Stop, Take control, edits and page disconnect bypass the ordinary operation lock. They revoke authority, advance cancellation epochs and signal cancellation before taking service/history locks, emit UP and read actual HID state, then enqueue evidence. A separate 200 ms watchdog checks exact process/window, geometry, permission, evidence health, the 3-second page lease and 180-second session limit. The independent input guardian protects registered pulses if the controller stalls or its transport closes. Release acknowledgment checks the pressed ledger **and** native key/button state, allowing at most 50 ms for queued UP. Unknown release blocks Start, connection changes and unsafe catalog switching.

Observation may capture an unobscured background game while Companion has focus. Execution requires the selected game foreground and unobscured throughout input. Only selected-process native chrome wholly inside its 32-pixel title bar is exempted from content occlusion; foreign overlays and content overlap remain refusals. Initial app-owned game placement preserves the exact 1280×1056 capture geometry and verifies PID/start/window before and after moving it; this preparation is not a gameplay skill.

Frames retain their real capture-start monotonic timestamp, pixels/hash, exact identity and geometry. Planning's old frame is never restamped. New captures after inference, on Review, before action, after pointer movement and after input re-ground the independent label and both balances. The model's target center must agree with unique independently detected text. Completion requires loan and cash both £100,000 → £90,000. A delivered action interrupted before completion remains partial and reports observed effects; Stop does not undo repayment. Those partial-result branches have simulated integration regressions and historical PB1 live evidence, not a new PB2 active-press interruption trial.

These are individual local observations, not percentile or release guarantees.

Model call/returned token information is retained per session. All unsuccessful probes remain retained.
