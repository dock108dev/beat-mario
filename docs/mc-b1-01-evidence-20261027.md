## Discovered limits and fixes

The first viewer run showed only sky: upstream 1.33.0 had a positive-Y camera
condition, 0–255 meshing loops, absolute section-array indices and face rejection
below zero. The local serving patches correct those for the qualified 1.21.4
Overworld and add camera following. This is a concrete compatibility adaptation,
not a claim that upstream worked unmodified. A short initial keepalive timeout
was also increased to 60 seconds; subsequent multi-minute sessions stayed live.

The first Stop sample included residual momentum and correctly returned
`unconfirmed`; the final check samples until a stable interval or a bounded
failure. No velocity teleport or forged success was used. Dig verification was
strengthened to require the server packet after inspection showed Mineflayer
predicts removal locally.

The harness, viewer and game endpoints remain loopback-only. Offline identities
are scoped to this new local world. No account credentials were accessed, no old
world or installed app was modified, and no legacy native-input feature was enabled.
